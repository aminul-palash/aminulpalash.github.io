### Beyond the model

I enjoy understanding how technical systems meet real people and real constraints. That curiosity leads me toward product thinking, system design, research reading, and mentoring engineers.

Outside day-to-day engineering, I make time for:

- Exploring new ideas in artificial intelligence and data science
- Reading research papers and following emerging AI methods
- Teaching, mentoring, and helping others build strong foundations
- Thinking about the social impact and responsible use of technology

The best work, for me, sits where deep technical craft meets useful human outcomes.
