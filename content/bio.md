I am **Md Aminul Haque Palash**, a Senior AI Engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I build production machine learning systems across recommendation, forecasting, conversational AI, edge inference, and multi-agent applications.

I currently work at **Foodi Express Limited**, where I develop personalized food and branch recommendations, delivery-time estimation, demand forecasting, and scalable data infrastructure for a large food delivery platform.

Previously, I spent more than six years at **BJIT Limited** building AI products for travel, food, mobile, and conversational experiences. My work included a LangGraph-based travel assistant, an on-device recommendation system, and an AI travel diary for iOS.
